Logistics

🚚 Delivery & Shipping

Within Nigeria

Standard delivery to all 36 states via GIG Logistics, DHL Nigeria, and Kwik. Estimated 2–5 working days depending on location. Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt get priority same-week delivery.

International Export

We ship to the UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Australia, and more via DHL Express and Aramex. Estimated 7–14 working days. All customs documentation handled by our export team.

Delivery Rates

  • Lagos / Abuja / PH: ₦2,500 – ₦4,000
  • Other Nigerian cities: ₦3,500 – ₦6,000
  • International: Calculated by weight & destination
  • Free delivery on orders above ₦100,000
Policy

🔄 Returns & Refunds

We stand behind every product. If something is wrong, we make it right.

When Can You Return?

  • Item received is different from what was ordered
  • Manufacturing defect or quality issue
  • Wrong size delivered (where applicable)
  • Item damaged in transit

Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery. Contact us via WhatsApp with photos of the item.

What Happens Next?

  • We review your photos within 24 hours
  • We arrange free pickup of the returned item
  • Replacement dispatched within 5 working days
  • Refund processed within 3–5 business days (bank transfer)

Custom-made or personalised orders are non-refundable unless defective.
