Our Story

Why We Built Aba Craft

Aba, Abia State has long been Africa's hidden manufacturing capital — home to tens of thousands of skilled artisans producing world-class leather shoes, garments, bags, furniture, and more. Yet for decades, this incredible talent remained largely invisible to the rest of the world.

Ariaria International Market — the heart of Aba's production economy — produces more handmade leather goods per day than many entire countries. Yet the artisans who power it rarely get the recognition, pricing, or platform they deserve.

Aba Craft was built to change that. We are a digital marketplace that elevates Aba's finest craftsmen — giving them a verified platform, connecting them to modern buyers, and ensuring every transaction is fair, transparent, and celebrated.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🎯

Authenticity

Every product on Aba Craft is genuinely handmade by verified Nigerian artisans. No replicas. No imports.

⭐

Quality First

We inspect every artisan before listing them. If it does not meet our standard, it does not reach our customers.

🤝

Fair Trade

Artisans earn fair prices. No middleman markup. Your money goes directly to the hands that made your product.

🌍

Global Reach

We believe Aba craftsmanship deserves a global audience. Our export arm ships to the UK, USA, Canada, and beyond.

Buyer Protection

Every purchase is protected. Not satisfied? We resolve it — full refund or replacement, no questions asked.

🌱

Community Impact
