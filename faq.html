Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, returns, and becoming an artisan on Aba Craft.

Ordering

📦 Orders & Products

Click "Order Now" on any product page — this opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to our team. Tell us your size, colour, and delivery address. Our team will confirm availability and payment details within 30 minutes during business hours.
Yes. Almost all our products are made-to-order, so customisation is standard. Simply mention your requirements in your WhatsApp message — specific colour, measurements, material, or finishing details. Our artisans will confirm feasibility and any additional cost.
Yes — 100%. Every product on Aba Craft is made by verified artisans based in Aba, Abia State. We inspect every artisan before listing them and perform quality checks on every order before dispatch. No imports. No replicas.
Standard items like shoes and sandals take 1–3 working days. Custom orders (bespoke suits, personalised bags) take 5–7 working days. Furniture pieces may take 7–14 working days depending on complexity. We will always give you an estimated timeline before you confirm your order.
We accept bank transfer (all Nigerian banks), pay on delivery (within Lagos, Abuja, and Aba), and we are working on card payment integration. For international orders, we accept USD/GBP bank transfer and PayPal (USD). Full payment details are shared via WhatsApp after you place your order.
Shipping

🚚 Delivery & Shipping

We deliver to all 36 states plus FCT Abuja. We use GIG Logistics, DHL Nigeria, and Kwik for domestic deliveries. Estimated delivery time is 2–5 working days after dispatch depending on your location.
Yes. We currently ship to the UK, USA, Canada, Germany, Italy, France, Australia, and several other countries. International delivery takes 7–14 working days via DHL Express or Aramex. All customs documentation and export paperwork is handled by our team.
Domestic delivery: ₦2,500–₦6,000 depending on your state. Lagos, Abuja, and PH have the lowest rates. International delivery is calculated by weight and destination — our team will quote you accurately via WhatsApp. Orders above ₦100,000 qualify for free domestic delivery.
Yes. Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp. You can track your shipment in real time on the courier's website. Our team also sends proactive updates at key stages of delivery.
After Purchase

🔄 Returns & Refunds

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the item is defective, damaged in transit, or significantly different from what was ordered. Contact us via WhatsApp with clear photos of the issue. We will arrange free pickup and send a replacement or full refund within 5 working days.
Custom and personalised orders (bespoke suits, engraved items, custom measurements) are non-refundable unless there is a clear manufacturing defect. If you have concerns about a custom item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
Once your return is approved, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days via bank transfer. International refunds may take 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
Artisans

🏪 For Artisans

Fill out the artisan application form on our Contact page. Our team will review your work and schedule a quality assessment. If approved, you will receive guidance on how to set up your profile and list your products — at no cost.
Listing on Aba Craft is completely free. We only charge a small platform commission (percentage of each sale) when you successfully make a sale. There are no upfront fees, subscriptions, or listing charges.
Artisan payments are processed weekly via bank transfer. Once an order is completed and the buyer confirms receipt, your earnings (after platform commission) are transferred within 3–5 business days.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is available on WhatsApp 7 days a week. We typically respond within 30 minutes.
